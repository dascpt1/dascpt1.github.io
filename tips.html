Serving & storage tips

  • Serve whites and rosé chilled, reds slightly below room temperature.
  • Open reds 30-60 minutes before drinking, or decant if young/tannic.
  • Store bottles on their side, away from light and heat.

Wine with casual food

Wine doesn't need a formal sit-down meal to earn its place - it can hold its own against pizza, tacos, burgers or a kebab too. A chilled sparkling wine or a light, fruity red with good acidity and not too much tannin or oak tends to work best - it cuts through rich or greasy toppings instead of fighting them.

Wine for Christmas dinner

Turkey is milder than people expect, but the trimmings aren't: stuffing, cranberry, pigs in blankets and rich gravy all on one plate. A medium-to-full red with plenty of ripe fruit and soft tannins copes with all of it better than a big, oaky blockbuster does. If you'd rather have white, go for something crisp and unoaked that won't drown out the turkey.

M&S Collection Châteauneuf-du-Pape

The red · Grenache, Syrah & Cinsault · Southern Rhône · 75cl

A classic Southern Rhône blend led by Grenache, which gives it ripe strawberry fruit, with a little clove spice and a dried-herb, garrigue edge. It's smooth rather than chewy, so it sits nicely alongside turkey and the trimmings. It's also good with roast beef, lamb or game if you're not doing the bird.

Châteauneuf is a step up from an everyday bottle, so it suits the one meal of the year when that feels justified. Open it an hour before dinner and serve it just below room temperature.

M&S Classics Chablis

The white · Chardonnay · Burgundy · 75cl

Chablis is Chardonnay without the oak and butter. Grown on the limestone soils of northern Burgundy, it comes out lean and dry, with lemon, green apple and a flinty, almost stony finish. That freshness keeps it from overpowering the turkey, and it's just as good with a smoked salmon starter.

M&S make it with the Chablis growers' co-operative, which they've worked with for over 40 years. Serve it well chilled, but take it out of the fridge ten minutes before pouring so the flavour comes through.

Supermarket recommendations

Sainsbury's

Watch for the Nectar "buy 3+, save 25%" offer on Taste the Difference wines - mix and match red, white, rosé or sparkling, up to 36 bottles, bottles over £6 only. Needs a Nectar card scanned or linked at checkout. Sainsbury's run this on and off rather than all the time, so check it's on before relying on it.

Tesco

Clubcard Prices run a "buy 6+, save 25%" deal on wine, Champagne and Prosecco fairly regularly - swipe or link your Clubcard at checkout to get it. Bottles over £6 only (NI and England), up to 36 bottles, 18+. Can be stacked with other wine offers, just watch the pre-discount price doesn't dip under the minimum. Check it's currently running before relying on it - Tesco run this on and off rather than continuously.

On now, until 30 November: the buy 6+, save 25% deal is running. Faustino V Reserva Rioja is also a Clubcard Price at £10.50 (normally £12.50), and Piccini Memoro Red (£9.75) counts towards the six.

Asda

Worth a look for everyday fizz - Freixenet Cordon Negro Brut (75cl) is currently on Rollback at £8.75, down from £10.75, and rates well (4.5★, 44 reviews). Solid, reliable Cava for topping up the fridge rather than a special-occasion bottle. Prices and Rollbacks change regularly, so check the current price before assuming it still holds.

Freixenet is a big Catalan producer based just north of Barcelona in Penedès, and Cordon Negro - instantly recognisable by its black bottle - has been their flagship Cava since 1974. It's genuinely the best-selling imported sparkling wine anywhere in the world, which is why you'll spot it on shelves from corner shops to supermarkets well beyond Spain. Nothing fancy, but a dependable, crowd-pleasing bottle for an easy celebration or a low-key date night in.
