Serving & storage tips

  • Serve whites and rosé chilled, reds slightly below room temperature.
  • Open reds 30-60 minutes before drinking, or decant if young/tannic.
  • Store bottles on their side, away from light and heat.

Wine with casual food

Wine doesn't need a formal sit-down meal to earn its place - it can hold its own against pizza, tacos, burgers or a kebab too. A chilled sparkling wine or a light, fruity red with good acidity and not too much tannin or oak tends to work best - it cuts through rich or greasy toppings instead of fighting them.

Supermarket recommendations

Sainsbury's

Watch for the Nectar "buy 3+, save 25%" offer on Taste the Difference wines - mix and match red, white, rosé or sparkling, up to 36 bottles, bottles over £6 only. Needs a Nectar card scanned or linked at checkout. Currently running until 13 October 2026 - check it's still live before relying on it.

Tesco

Clubcard Prices run a "buy 6+, save 25%" deal on wine, Champagne and Prosecco fairly regularly - swipe or link your Clubcard at checkout to get it. Bottles over £6 only (NI and England), up to 36 bottles, 18+. Can be stacked with other wine offers, just watch the pre-discount price doesn't dip under the minimum. Check it's currently running before relying on it - Tesco run this on and off rather than continuously.

Asda

Worth a look for everyday fizz - Freixenet Cordon Negro Brut (75cl) is currently on Rollback at £8.75, down from £10.75, and rates well (4.5★, 44 reviews). Solid, reliable Cava for topping up the fridge rather than a special-occasion bottle. Prices and Rollbacks change regularly, so check the current price before assuming it still holds.

Freixenet is a big Catalan producer based just north of Barcelona in Penedès, and Cordon Negro - instantly recognisable by its black bottle - has been their flagship Cava since 1974. It's genuinely the best-selling imported sparkling wine anywhere in the world, which is why you'll spot it on shelves from corner shops to supermarkets well beyond Spain. Nothing fancy, but a dependable, crowd-pleasing bottle for an easy celebration or a low-key date night in.
