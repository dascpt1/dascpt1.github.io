How it works

Members

Members set up a monthly subscription and create their own Wine Bank.

Savings

Members purchase wine from various websites in bulk to save on shipping and transportation costs.

En Primeur wines

Opportunities to purchase wines early while still in the barrel, securing premium vintages before they are bottled and released to the market.
