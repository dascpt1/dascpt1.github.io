Placing an order

  1. Check the offers on any of the recommended websites below.
  2. Select the wines you would like to buy.
  3. Decide how many bottles you would like to order and from which site.
  4. Complete the order form below and click the "Email this order" button.

Order form

Fill this in and it'll open an email to mdwc@btinternet.com with your order ready to send - nothing is submitted from this page itself.

Payment

Members use the funds saved in their Wine Bank to pay for any purchases.

Do not put account numbers, sort codes, or any payment details on the order form - share these using the Wine Club WhatsApp group.
