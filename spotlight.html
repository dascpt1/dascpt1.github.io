Antídoto 2024

Tempranillo, D.O. Ribera del Duero, Castilla y León · £££

Bodegas Antídoto was founded by Bertrand Sourdais (of Bordeaux background) and David Hernando, working old, ecologically farmed Tempranillo vines - some 60 to 80 years old - at around 1000m in Soria. The name is deliberate: an "antidote" to the bigger, more concentrated style that used to define Ribera del Duero.

Briefly oak-aged rather than heavily so, it's silky and approachable rather than a blockbuster - toasted notes and structured but not overbearing tannins. Good with white meats, pasta or game, or just an easy bottle to open with friends. 92 Parker, 93 Decántalo, 91 Tim Atkin, 91 Suckling.

Barón de Ley Gran Reserva 2019

Tempranillo/Graciano, D.O.Ca. Rioja · ££

A classic Rioja Gran Reserva from one of the region's best-known producers - long aged (24 months in French and American oak, then another 36 months in bottle before release), which is where that typical Rioja character of ripe red fruit, tobacco and a soft balsamic edge comes from.

Rounded, elegant and easy-drinking for something this traditionally styled - a reliable choice if you want a "proper" Rioja without overthinking it. 91 Peñín.

Barón de Ley 3 Viñas Blanco Reserva 2021

Viura, Garnacha Blanca & Malvasía, D.O.Ca. Rioja · ££

An aged white Rioja, which is a bit of an oddity - most Rioja blanco is drunk young and unoaked. This one blends three grapes grown separately in three vineyards along the Najerilla valley, then spends around a year in American oak.

Expect nutty, honeyed notes alongside orchard fruit (pear, ripe apple) and a saline, mineral finish - good with richer fish or poultry rather than a light salad. 92 Decanter, 91 Suckling, 90 Peñín.
