This month's picks

Recaredo L'Infinit Brut Nature

Corpinnat, Alt Penedès, Catalonia · £££

Recaredo is a small, biodynamic house in Sant Sadurní d'Anoia - one of the growers who broke away from the Cava DO to found Corpinnat, a stricter designation for traditional-method sparkling wine. L'Infinit is one of their top cuvées: a Brut Nature (zero dosage, completely dry) blend of Xarel·lo, Macabeo, white Garnacha, Malvasia de Sitges and Xarel·lo Vermell, given extended ageing on its lees for extra depth and complexity.

It's organic, vegan and entirely handcrafted, and the flavour builds as you drink it - ripe white fruit and citrus give way to smoky notes and a chalky minerality that speaks to the terroir. Fine bubbles, bone dry, closer to grower Champagne than typical Cava. Good with seafood or aged cheese, or on its own as an aperitif.

Lazuli 2021 · Aquitania

Cabernet Sauvignon, Valle del Maipo, Chile · £££

Aquitania was founded by two well-regarded names from the Bordeaux wine world, and its Lazuli bottling is one of the wines that put Chile's Maipo Valley on the map for serious Cabernet Sauvignon. Expect dark fruit - black cherry and plum - alongside mocha, tobacco and a long, structured finish. A good choice for a dinner party where you want something to impress.

On Vine Pinot Noir 2024 · Van Loveren

Pinot Noir, South Africa · ££

An easy-drinking, well-balanced Pinot Noir from South Africa, with black cherry and ripe strawberry fruit and a light spiced finish. Doesn't need a special occasion - good alongside something comforting like a cottage pie or a leg of lamb.
