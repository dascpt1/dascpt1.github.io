Week of 9 Sep 2026

This week: Recaredo L'Infinit Brut Nature

Recaredo is a small, biodynamic house in Sant Sadurní d'Anoia, Alt Penadès (Catalonia) - one of the growers who broke away from the Cava DO to found Corpinnat, a stricter designation for traditional-method sparkling wine. L'Infinit is one of their top cuvées: a Brut Nature (zero dosage, completely dry) blend of Xarel·lo, Macabeo, white Garnacha, Malvasia de Sitges and Xarel·lo Vermell, given extended ageing on its lees for extra depth and complexity.

It's organic, vegan and entirely handcrafted, and the flavour builds as you drink it - ripe white fruit and citrus give way to smoky notes and a chalky minerality that speaks to the Alt Penedès terroir. Fine bubbles, bone dry, closer to grower Champagne than typical Cava. Good with seafood or aged cheese, or on its own as an aperitif.
