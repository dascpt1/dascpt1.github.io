December 2026

This month's picks

Ten recommendations from Decántalo for the festive season: seven reds and three whites, from everyday bottles to a special-occasion treat.

Reds

Ultreia Saint Jacques 2023

Mencía, D.O. Bierzo · ££

From Raúl Pérez, the winemaker who did much to put Bierzo on the map. Mencía from old hillside vines, made in a light, fresh style – bright red fruit and a lifted, almost floral feel rather than weight. A good everyday red that's easy with food. Sustainably farmed.

Buy from Decántalo →

Casa Castillo Monastrell 2024

Monastrell, D.O. Jumilla · ££

Jumilla used to mean big, hot, jammy reds; Casa Castillo is one of the estates showing what Monastrell can do with a lighter touch. Ripe dark fruit but fresh and juicy with it. Worth decanting for half an hour. Eco-friendly farming.

Buy from Decántalo →

Château Grange Cochard Les Charmes 2020

Gamay, Morgon (Beaujolais cru) · ££

Proper cru Beaujolais from the Les Charmes slope in Morgon, one of the village's most highly regarded soils. More depth than most people expect from Gamay – red cherry and raspberry with an elegant, silky finish. Lovely lightly chilled with charcuterie or roast chicken.

Out of stock on the UK site when checked on 25 November 2026 – check before ordering.

Buy from Decántalo →

Viña Ardanza Reserva 2020

Tempranillo & Garnacha, D.O.Ca. Rioja · £££

La Rioja Alta's flagship Reserva and about as classic as Rioja gets: long ageing in American oak gives the familiar vanilla, spice and dried red fruit, with Garnacha adding warmth. A great choice for a Christmas roast. Decant it. Vegan.

Buy from Decántalo →

The Chocolate Block 2024

Syrah-led blend, Swartland, South Africa · £££

Boekenhoutskloof's cult blend – mostly Syrah with Grenache, Cinsault, Cabernet Sauvignon and a dash of Viognier. Dark fruit, pepper and spice, rich but not heavy. A crowd-pleaser with barbecue, lamb or a winter stew.

Buy from Decántalo →

El Enemigo Malbec 2023

Malbec, Mendoza, Argentina · £££

From Alejandro Vigil and Adrianna Catena – a fresher, more playful take on Mendoza Malbec than the big oaky style. Plum and violet with lively acidity. Obvious with steak, but good with anything off the grill. Sustainably farmed.

Buy from Decántalo →

Comando G Rozas 1er Cru 2024

Garnacha, D.O. Vinos de Madrid (Sierra de Gredos) · ££££

The treat of the month. Comando G are the names behind the Gredos Garnacha revival, farming high-altitude vines on granite biodynamically. Pale, delicate and very mineral – closer to fine Burgundy than to typical Spanish Garnacha. One to save for a special occasion.

Buy from Decántalo →

Whites

Esporão Reserva Branco 2024

Antão Vaz, Arinto & Roupeiro, DOC Alentejo, Portugal · ££

A benchmark Portuguese white from one of the Alentejo's leading estates, blending three local grapes and aged on its lees. Citrus and stone fruit with a creamy texture and a fresh finish. Very good with fish, chicken or a seafood rice. Eco-friendly.

Buy from Decántalo →

Rafael Palacios Louro Godello 2025

Godello, D.O. Valdeorras · £££

Rafael Palacios is the go-to name for Godello, and Louro is his more approachable bottling. Barrel-fermented, but the oak is in the background – pear, white flowers and a mineral, saline edge. A serious white for the price and a good alternative to white Burgundy.

Buy from Decántalo →

Barco del Corneta 2024

Verdejo, VT Castilla y León · £££

A small-production Verdejo from old vines near Rueda, a long way from the cheap and cheerful versions in the supermarket. Aged partly in wood, it has more texture and depth than you'd expect, with herbs, citrus and a long, savoury finish. Eco-friendly and sustainably farmed.

Buy from Decántalo →
